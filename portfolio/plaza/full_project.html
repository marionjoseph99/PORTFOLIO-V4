Jagna De Plaza

Historic Town Center Civic Plaza Revitalization • Población, Jagna, Bohol • 5,200 sqm

[DESIGN CONCEPT & SPATIAL STRATEGY]

A 5,200 sqm comprehensive civic plaza revitalization study located in Población, Jagna, Bohol, focused on public gathering, civic pride, and shaded outdoor tropical comfort. The proposal reimagines the historic town center into an active civic living room capable of hosting cultural celebrations, municipal events, and local heritage weekend markets.

Through strategic deployment of modular seating, geometric canopy structures, pedestrianized promenades, and central water monument anchor nodes, the plaza encourages community interaction while creating a distinct sense of civic identity.
