ARCHITECTURE • TERMINAL DESIGN • YEAR 2024

Layag Airport

Sangley Point Domestic Airport • Sangley Point, Cavite City • 147 Hectares

[DESIGN CONCEPT & SPATIAL STRATEGY]

A 147-hectare conceptual domestic airport terminal proposal located at Sangley Point, Cavite City that harmonizes passenger movement, structural sail canopies, and visual clarity. Inspired by traditional Filipino seafaring vessels ('Layag'), the aerodynamic roof profiles scoop indirect natural illumination into terminal departure halls while shading drop-off lanes.

The spatial program organizes multi-level vertical circulation separating arrival passenger streams from departures check-in and gate boarding piers, streamlining apron transit and ensuring passenger security across the extensive coastal airport grounds.
