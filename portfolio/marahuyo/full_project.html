Marahuyo Park

Calamba Baywalk Waterfront Promenade • Calamba Baywalk, Calamba City • 1.5 Hectares

[DESIGN CONCEPT & SPATIAL STRATEGY]

'Marahuyo' (an ancient Tagalog word meaning 'to be enchanted') is a 1.5-hectare comprehensive urban waterfront revitalization strategy along the Calamba Baywalk in Calamba City, reclaiming the shorefront into a resilient ecological public park.

By combining stepped bioswales, retention wetland zones, and multi-tier pedestrian boardwalks across 1.5 hectares, the park acts as both a civic community amenity and an active flood buffer that adapts dynamically to lake water level fluctuations.

[PROJECT SPECS]

LOCATION: Calamba Baywalk, Calamba City
